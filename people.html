Join us

Open positions. I am looking to build the lab with PhD students interested in experiments and simplifying the physics. While a background in Fluid Mechanics, Thermodynamics, or Heat Transfer will definitely help in getting used to the literature, willingness to learn new things and to use the knowledge in uncharted territories is far more valuable. The lab is always open for Undergrads. Email me or drop by my office if you are interested in learning what we do.

What you would work on. You will plan and build experiments to understand complex multiscalar physicochemical hydrodynamical systems. Almost all the projects we would undertake are going to be interdisciplinary, drawing ideas from fluid mechanics, solid mechanics, and physical sciences. See our research.

How to apply. Email {{ site.email }} with your CV, a short summary of past projects (if any), and a paragraph on what kind of research (need not be from our lab!) interests you and why. Subject: "Prospective: your name".
