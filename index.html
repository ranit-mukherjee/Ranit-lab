How we work

01

Identify

We're a curiosity-driven lab in an engineering department. We start with real challenges in natural or engineered soft systems, aiming for practical solutions but always chasing the elegant physics underneath. Since these problems often stray into biology, environmental physics, or chemical engineering (a little epistemic trespassing, perhaps), we brainstorm together, read broadly to form an informed perspective, and run quick dimensional (scaling) analyses to identify the key parameters for the next stage.

02

Table-top analog

This is the heart of the lab. We create model experimental set-ups for the real-world systems we are studying. This helps in creating a controlled environment where we can manipulate parameters and record the corresponding changes in high spatio-temporal resolution.

03

Physics

The longest stage. With all the experimental observation (quantitative and qualitative), we now try to make sense of the physical mechanisms using minimal mathematical models, such as scaling laws and asymptotic analysis.
